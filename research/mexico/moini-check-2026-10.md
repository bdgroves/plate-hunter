# Mexico cross-check against mexicoplates.moini.net (Oct 27, 2026)

Jim Moini's collector site (last updated June 14, 2026). **Its images and text may not be reused**, so it
is used here only as a second opinion on which designs exist and their years. Nothing in the game was
changed from it. A plate should still be confirmed on an official source before it goes into the game.

Moini's years come from the dates under his photos; open-ended ranges ("2025-") mean still being issued.
