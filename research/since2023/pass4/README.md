# Fourth pass (October 2026): catalogs read in a real browser

TX, AZ, MS, KY, GA, MT, MD, VA and IL keep their plate catalogs behind scripts that plain fetches
can't run, so this pass read each one in Chrome, compared the game against the full current list,
and marked the state "complete".

Held back by hand before applying:
- TX: Grambling State, Choose Life, Panoramic and plain Lone Star are missing from My Plates but
  were not marked retired (listed under "retired_unsure"); absence alone isn't proof.
- MS: retired-military, National Guard and Ambulance plates are qualification plates the public
  gallery may simply not show, so only Blair E Batson (replaced) was retired. Juvenile Diabetes and
  Merchant Marine were left out as likely versions of plates already in the game.
- KY: Friends of Hemp and Cardinal were not retired.
- MT: Butte Silver Bow Government left out.
- MD: "retired" means the plate is missing from MVA's full 777-entry organizational list. Military
  medal plates were not retired because MVA's military gallery omits the statutory ones.
